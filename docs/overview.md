 Ionic Payments enables you to collect payment from your customers securely and efficiently, using a drop-in solution powered by the most trusted providers by consumers worldwide: Google Pay

__Don't have an Google Pay subscription?__ [Try it free now](http://dashboard.ionicframework.com/personal/apps?native_trial=1).

## Why Google Pay?

Ionic Payments brings the benefits of Google Pay into a single cross-platform API, allowing you to reach all your customers while saving time and valuable developer resources.

Payments is the only enterprise-grade solution backed by world-class support and services, featuring a guaranteed SLA and helpdesk support. Enjoy peace of mind knowing you can focus on your app’s unique features with ongoing support for the latest versions of Google Pay and mobile operating systems.
