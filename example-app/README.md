# Example App Configuration

This example app demonstrates the Google Pay plugin.

## Setup

Before running the app, update the Google Pay configuration in `src/pages/Home.tsx`:

1. Replace `REPLACE_WITH_GATEWAY` with your payment gateway.
2. Replace `REPLACE_WITH_GATEWAY_MERCHANT_ID` with your merchant ID at the gateway.
3. Replace `REPLACE_WITH_MERCHANT_ID` with your Google Pay merchant ID (obtained via the Google Pay Business Console).
4. Replace `REPLACE_WITH_MERCHANT_NAME` with your merchant name.
